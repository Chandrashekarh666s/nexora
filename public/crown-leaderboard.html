Leaderboard

Every crown is driven only by live performance data. The gold rises and drains as results change. (Demo feed: sample students.)

Plug in your backend: LeaderboardCrown.render(studentId, { pillars: { correctness, timeliness, consistency } }) on any element with data-crown="studentId", or give raw numbers { testsPassed, testsTotal, timeUsedSec, timeTargetSec, streakDays }. 100% needs Consistency + Correctness + Timeliness together.
